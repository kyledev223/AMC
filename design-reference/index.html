The Airdrop

The Cult pairs its own $AMC memecoin with the real thing. Holders receive tokenized AMC Entertainment shares, issued on Solana by a regulated tokenized-stock provider.

    Any Solana wallet counts. Coins sitting on an exchange don't.

  2. The Council takes a snapshot

    Snapshot dates are announced on X and Telegram ahead of time.

  3. Tokenized AMC lands in your wallet

    Shares are split between holders in proportion to how much $AMC each wallet held at the snapshot.

Full airdrop terms, eligible regions and the share issuer are posted here before the first snapshot. Tokenized stocks aren't available in every country, including the United States.
